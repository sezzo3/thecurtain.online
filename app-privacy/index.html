Scroll the Fables — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version: Scroll the Fables collects no personal data. No account, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics. What your child reads stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app works entirely on your device:

  • No account or sign-up. The app opens straight to the stories.
  • No data collection. We do not collect names, ages, locations, contacts, photos, browsing habits or anything else.
  • No advertising and no tracking. There are no ad networks, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers in the app.
  • Offline by design. The stories and study guides are stored inside the app and read without any internet connection.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud normally plays ElevenLabs recordings included with the app. If a recording is unavailable or cannot play, the app uses your device’s built-in speech voice. The app does not send story text or audio to ElevenLabs or any other service during playback.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions (monthly and yearly) and a one-time lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's own privacy policy covers purchase processing. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device.

Children

Scroll the Fables does not collect children’s data and has no chat or user-generated content. The purchase screen links to this policy and the terms of use on The Curtain website.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that needs data), we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email legal@thecurtain.online.
