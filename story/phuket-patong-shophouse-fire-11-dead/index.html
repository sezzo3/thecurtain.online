Fire Tears Through Phuket Shophouse, Killing 11 and Injuring 16

The Curtain Editorial · 2026-11-16 · 5 min read

A fire tore through a commercial shophouse in the resort town of Patong on Thailand's island of Phuket early on Monday, killing 11 people and injuring 16, police said. The blaze broke out at about 3:17 a.m. local time in a building housing a motorcycle rental business, rented accommodation and a restaurant, and spread to several neighbouring buildings before firefighters brought it under control about two hours later.

Most of the dead are believed to have died from smoke inhalation after flames and thick black smoke filled the upper floors, officials said. Police put the number of injured at 16 — 15 Thai nationals and one Myanmar national — and said no tourists were among those killed or injured. Thai media reported higher figures, with some outlets saying as many as 36 to 38 people were treated in hospital, mostly for smoke inhalation.

The fire started on the ground floor, where the rental business kept more than 100 motorcycles alongside stored fuel, engine oil, tyres and plastic parts, according to Patong officials. Those combustible materials fed the flames and produced dense, choking smoke that filled the rented rooms above within minutes, trapping people who were asleep and blocking the way down.

The cause remains under investigation, but early findings point to an electrical fault. Phuket's governor said an initial investigation found that a short circuit probably occurred on a utility pole in front of the shop during a rainstorm, and officials said the fuel, tyres and plastic parts stored with the motorcycles helped the flames spread with unusual speed.

Rescue teams searched the charred building for people feared trapped inside, and the bodies of the dead were taken to Vachira Phuket Hospital for autopsy and formal identification. Authorities have not released the names of the dead, and it is not yet clear whether safety lapses contributed to the toll. More than 100 motorcycles parked on the ground floor were destroyed, along with the rental business itself.

Patong, on Phuket's western coast, is one of Thailand's busiest beach resorts, and the blaze came four months after a fire at a Bangkok bar killed at least 27 people. The investigation into the Patong fire continues, and officials say further details will follow as police and fire investigators complete their examination of the site.
