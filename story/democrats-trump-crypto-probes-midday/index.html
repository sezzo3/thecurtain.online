After the Blue Wave, Democrats Turn Their Sights on the Trump Family's Crypto Fortune

The Curtain Editorial · 2026-11-08 · 3 min read

The Democrats' sweep of both chambers of Congress in Tuesday's midterm elections has set up a reckoning for the cryptocurrency industry — and for the Trump family's own crypto empire, which Democratic lawmakers now plan to investigate with the full subpoena power of Congress.

Advertisement

Democrats won control of the House of Representatives and the Senate on 3 November, and while the new Congress does not convene until January 2027, the party has already begun staffing up for oversight. Incoming House Oversight Committee chair Robert Garcia of California responded to the results with a two-word warning to the president — "Lawyer up" — and his office has outlined plans to investigate alleged corruption involving the Trump administration and businesses connected to the president's relatives. Senator Elizabeth Warren of Massachusetts, expected to chair the Senate Banking Committee, and Representative Maxine Waters, tipped to lead the House Financial Services Committee, are set to run parallel inquiries into the sector.

At the top of the list is World Liberty Financial, the cryptocurrency company associated with the Trump family, along with its dollar-pegged stablecoin USD1 and the TRUMP memecoin. Lawmakers are expected to examine a reported $500 million investment by a vehicle linked to Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan, the UAE's national security adviser, which acquired a 49% stake in the company shortly before Donald Trump's inauguration in January 2025. Reuters has reported that 75% of the proceeds from sales of WLFI tokens go to the Trump family, and has estimated that the family's cryptocurrency ventures have generated at least $2.3 billion in earnings while investors in the associated tokens suffered substantial losses.

The inquiries are also expected to reach into the regulators themselves. Crypto entrepreneur Justin Sun bought $75 million of WLFI tokens, and in February 2025, shortly after the Trump administration took office, the Securities and Exchange Commission paused a civil case against him. Democrats are also expected to examine the October 2025 pardon of Binance founder Changpeng Zhao, and whether gaps in anti-money-laundering enforcement let Trump-linked ventures profit from illicit activity — a question Warren raised in a Senate inquiry letter she signed with Adam Schiff.

The industry's mood has already shifted from lobbying to damage control. The Republican-written Clarity Act, a market-structure bill the sector had championed, died in September when a Senate procedural vote failed 49 to 50, after Democrats demanded stricter limits on presidential crypto holdings, including forced divestment above a set value. The TRUMP memecoin fell more than 15% as the midterm result sank in, and with no committee having yet announced a hearing date or issued a subpoena, the reckoning for now is a forecast — but one the market is already pricing in.

The reaction among Democratic supporters online has been jubilant and vengeful in equal measure. A thread on the Democrats winning the Arizona House, Senate and governor's office for the first time since 1966 drew more than 16,000 upvotes on r/news, while a separate r/politics thread on Mr Trump sharing posts questioning the midterm results drew more than 5,300 upvotes within hours. Whether the investigations produce evidence of wrongdoing or become a partisan standoff, they will dominate Washington's agenda from January — and they mark the end of the crypto industry's honeymoon with the Trump White House.
