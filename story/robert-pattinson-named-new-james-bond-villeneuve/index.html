The Name's Pattinson: Robert Pattinson Is the New James Bond

The Curtain Editorial · 2026-11-17 · 5 min read

Robert Pattinson will be the next James Bond, Amazon MGM Studios announced on Monday, ending five years of speculation over who would succeed Daniel Craig as 007. The 40-year-old British actor will make his debut in the still-untitled 26th film in the series, directed by Denis Villeneuve from a script by Steven Knight, the creator of Peaky Blinders. The announcement came in a short teaser video showing Pattinson in a tuxedo, gun in hand, as the series' theme played and the words "James Bond will return" appeared on screen. "James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime," Pattinson said in a statement.

The casting is the first great decision of Amazon's Bond era. Amazon paid more than $8.56 billion for MGM, the franchise's parent company, in 2022, and last year finally took creative control of 007 under a new joint venture with longtime rights holders Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson. Villeneuve, the French-Canadian director behind the Dune trilogy and Arrival, said he was "overjoyed" with the choice; Amy Pascal and David Heyman are producing. The Royal Family also commented on the casting, a rare intervention in a Hollywood hiring decision. No release date, plot details or supporting cast have been announced, and the roles of M, Q and the Bond girl are still to be filled.

The search for Craig's successor was the most watched casting contest in Hollywood. Pattinson was not publicly known to be a contender until 6 November, when The Hollywood Reporter revealed he had screen-tested for the part, and he beat a shortlist reported to include Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner, Jacob Elordi, Paul Mescal and Harris Dickinson, who tested in London in late October. Turner had been wrongly reported as the choice on 3 November. Pattinson is reported to be locked into a three-picture deal, according to Puck News. He had first expressed interest in the role 14 years ago, telling The Sun in 2012 that he would like to play 007 one day — though not any time soon.

Pattinson becomes the seventh actor to play Bond on the big screen, following Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Craig, whose five-film run from Casino Royale in 2006 to No Time to Die in 2021 ended with Bond's death. He is the first new Bond since Craig took the role in 2006, and the choice breaks with the franchise's habit of casting a relative unknown: Pattinson became a global star as Edward Cullen in Twilight and has since built one of the most respected careers of his generation, including the title role in The Batman in 2022. His 2026 alone has included The Odyssey, The Drama and Primetime — and next month he appears in Villeneuve's Dune: Part Three.

The reaction has been warm, if not unanimous. On Reddit's r/JamesBond, former Bond George Lazenby welcomed Pattinson to "the most exclusive club in the world", a post that drew more than 2,600 upvotes, and fans noted that Pattinson had named Lazenby's On Her Majesty's Secret Service as his favourite Bond film in a 2012 interview. On Hacker News, commenters broadly backed the choice — several noting that the doubts are smaller than those that greeted Craig's casting in 2005 — though some were disappointed that the studio broke with the tradition of casting an unknown, reading it as a sign of limited faith in the script and the brand alone.

What happens next is production, which is due to begin soon, and the long wait for a release date: the film is unlikely to reach cinemas before 2028. The questions that will decide whether the gamble pays off are the ones no casting announcement can answer — whether Villeneuve and Knight can give the first Amazon Bond a story worthy of the franchise's 60-year history, and whether an actor famous since his teens can disappear into a character who has swallowed bigger stars before him. For now, the guessing game that has consumed Bond fans since 2021 is over. The name is Pattinson — Robert Pattinson.
