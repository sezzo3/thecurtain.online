AI use and human responsibility

Updated: 25 November 2026

Sezgin Baktiaya writes The Curtain’s articles himself. He draws on his IT background to use AI for research and translation, with a focus on credible sources. Editorial responsibility and publication decisions remain with him.

Limits and editorial standards

AI output can contain factual errors, fabricated references or inaccurate translations. It is not evidence in itself. The editorial standard is to check claims against the cited material, make uncertainty clear and correct errors when identified. AI assistance is not a claim that a story has been independently verified.

Images and attribution

Generated illustrations should be identified as illustrations and should not be presented as documentary photographs of real events. Quotes and interviews must come from identifiable sources; AI output must not be attributed to a real person as something they said.

Questions and corrections

To question a claim, translation or image, send the article URL, the specific passage and any supporting evidence through the contact page. Sezgin Baktiaya is responsible for reviewing editorial concerns.
